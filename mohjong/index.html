How to play

Connect

Tap two matching tiles. They clear if a line can join them with at most two turns, through empty space. The line may go around the outside of the board.

Solitaire

A tile can move when nothing covers it and its left or right side is open. Dim tiles are blocked for now.

Glow

Match again within three seconds and your lantern glows for bonus points. Purely a bonus, never needed.

Stars

Three stars for a win with no hint and no shuffle. Two for one of them. One for any win. Stars never block progress.

Never

No timer. No lives. Every board can be finished.
